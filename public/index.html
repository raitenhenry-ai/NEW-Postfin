FAQ

Straight answers about how Postfin works.

What does Postfin do?

Postfin turns your product into short-form content, then schedules and posts it across your connected social accounts — so you are not writing, editing, and uploading by hand every day.

Which platforms can I post to?

Connect Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and more from one place. Pick the accounts you want for each video and Postfin publishes to them on your schedule.

Do I still need to make the videos myself?

No. Give Postfin a product link or a brief and it generates slideshows or avatar videos, captions, and a posting plan. You can edit anything before it goes live.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month to month — cancel whenever you want and you keep access through the end of your billing period.

What’s the difference between plans?

Starter covers core slideshows and scheduling. Growth raises volume and accounts for brands posting consistently. Scale adds AI video generation and multi-brand workspaces.

Will my content look AI-generated?

You control tone, angle, and captions, and you can rewrite or regenerate anything before it posts. The goal is on-brand short-form that fits each platform — not generic filler.
